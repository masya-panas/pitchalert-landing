2. Description of the service

PitchAlert is a Chrome browser extension that provides:

  • A live countdown to the next match of your chosen national team in the 2026 FIFA World Cup.
  • The match kickoff time converted to your local timezone.
  • Chrome browser reminders (24 h, 3 h, and 30 min before kickoff) and a toolbar badge.
  • Pro features (when purchased): multi-team following, full-tournament dashboard, smart custom reminders, on-screen kickoff alert, where-to-watch broadcaster links, and calendar export.

The match schedule bundled in the Extension is based on the official FIFA World Cup 2026 fixture list. While we take care to keep it accurate, we cannot guarantee the schedule is always up to date in the event of FIFA rescheduling or force-majeure cancellations.
